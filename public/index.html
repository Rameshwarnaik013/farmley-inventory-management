DOH Determination Methodology

Analytically Driven Calculation Framework for Finished Goods Inventory Holding Duration

1 ABC Classification (Pareto Analysis)

SKUs are ranked by Annual Usage Value = Units Sold x MRP and classified into three tiers:

ClassCumulative ValueTypical % of SKUsControl Level
ATop 80%~15%Tight (highest service level)
B80% - 95%~17%Moderate
C95% - 100%~68%Lean stocking

Data source: abc_summary sheet — ranked by Usage = Units x MRP

2 Service Level & Z-Score Assignment

Each ABC class is assigned a target service level (probability demand is met from stock during lead time). The corresponding Z-score from the standard normal distribution:

Z-scores are configurable inputs. Higher Z = more safety stock = higher service level.

3 Safety Stock Calculation

Step 1: Convert monthly variability to daily

sigma_daily = sigma_monthly / sqrt(30)

Step 2: Safety Stock

Safety_Stock = Z x sigma_daily x sqrt(Lead_Time_days)

sigma_monthly = STDEV of last 6 months demand (rolling window). Lead Time = 2 days (time to process, pack & move to FG warehouse; production is continuous/daily).

4 DOH Determination (Analytically Derived)

MIN Inventory (Reorder Point)

MIN = (Daily_Demand x Lead_Time) + Safety_Stock

MAX Inventory

MAX = MIN + Order_Quantity   (where Order_Qty = Daily_Demand x Order_Cycle_Days, default 1 day)

Average Inventory

AVG = (MAX + MIN) / 2

Days of Holding (DOH)

DOH_MIN = MIN_Inventory / Daily_Demand   ← minimum holding days at reorder point

DOH_MAX = MAX_Inventory / Daily_Demand   ← maximum days post-replenishment

DOH_AVG = AVG_Inventory / Daily_Demand   ← expected holding at any given time

DOH is not based on judgment. It is a direct mathematical consequence of the safety stock model, service level targets, and observed demand variability.

5 KGS Conversion & Cross-Validation

Conversion

Any_KGS_metric = Units_metric x Conversion_Factor (kg/unit)

Cross-validation

DOH computed in Units basis = DOH computed in KGS basis (algebraically identical)

Conversion factors sourced from BOM / Sales Projection file. KGS view enables warehouse-level capacity planning.

6 Actual vs Recommended DOH Comparison

Actual DOH (Observed)

Actual_DOH = Closing_Stock_KGS (latest month) / Daily_Demand_KGS

Variance & Status

DOH_Variance = Actual_DOH - Recommended_DOH_AVG

Status = EXCESS if Actual > DOH_MAX  |  OPTIMAL if between MIN-MAX  |  BELOW ROP if Actual < DOH_MIN
